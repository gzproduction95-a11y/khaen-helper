# Changelog

Notable project changes are recorded here.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-09-16

### Added

- Major-key display that maps the physical pipe pitches to the relative natural-minor layout while showing scale degrees and chord choices in the selected major key.
- Responsive presentation that centers the chord information between the chord panel and Khaen diagram in landscape, and gives the diagram more room in portrait.
- Project overview, development/deployment instructions, contribution guidance, and page metadata.

### Improved

- Instrument diagram scales to its available area, including pipe labels and top-bracket clearance.
- Settings panel scrolling and workspace sizing across short, portrait, and landscape viewports.
